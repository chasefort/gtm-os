## Brand Position

NorthstarIQ should sound like an operationally mature AI company for regulated revenue teams. The tone should be calm, precise, and commercially useful. The product should never sound like it is trying to make compliance disappear.

The brand should help buyers feel:
- This team understands regulated sales and marketing workflows.
- This product respects compliance review.
- The AI-assisted outputs can be inspected.
- The workflow helps marketing and sales move faster without creating uncontrolled risk.

## Voice Principles

### Clear

Use plain language. Avoid vague AI language like "unlock intelligence" unless paired with a concrete workflow.

Preferred:
- "Generate cited battlecards from approved sources."
- "Flag unsupported claims before review."
- "Give sellers reusable approved language."

Avoid:
- "Transform your content universe."
- "Revolutionize compliant growth."
- "AI magic for regulated teams."

### Calm

Regulated buyers do not want hype. They want control, traceability, and operational confidence.

Preferred:
- "Keep compliance in control."
- "Make review easier to inspect."
- "Preserve source citations."

Avoid:
- "Move 10x faster with zero risk."
- "Eliminate compliance bottlenecks."
- "Guarantee compliant content."

### Specific

Name the workflow, user, and output whenever possible.

Preferred:
- "Advisor social posts."
- "Branch campaign copy."
- "Sales objection handlers."
- "Product launch messaging."

Avoid:
- "Content."
- "Assets."
- "Knowledge."
- "AI output."

### Human-in-the-Loop

The brand must reinforce that compliance and subject-matter experts stay involved.

Preferred:
- "Review-ready."
- "Approved source packs."
- "Human approval remains in the workflow."

Avoid:
- "Fully automated review."
- "No legal team required."
- "Publish instantly."

## Approved Phrases

- "Governed revenue knowledge for regulated teams."
- "Source-grounded content with citations and review trails."
- "Reduce review cycles while keeping compliance in control."
- "Reusable approved language for sales, marketing, and customer-facing teams."
- "A source of truth that sellers, marketers, and compliance can inspect."
- "Review-ready outputs grounded in approved sources."
- "Cited AI outputs for regulated revenue workflows."

## Avoided Phrases

Do not use:
- "Automates compliance review."
- "Eliminates compliance bottlenecks."
- "Guarantees compliant content."
- "Replaces legal review."
- "Lets advisors publish without approval."
- "One-click compliance."
- "Risk-free AI content."

## Messaging Pillars

### Alignment

One source of truth across marketing, sales, customer success, compliance, and leadership.

Proof language:
- Source packs.
- Cited answers.
- Alignment checks.
- Contradiction detection.

### Trust

Every output should show where claims came from and whether the source is approved.

Proof language:
- Citations.
- Approval labels.
- Source categories.
- Review trails.

### Speed

Teams can move faster when they reuse approved language and catch unsupported claims earlier.

Proof language:
- Faster review cycles.
- Fewer rewrite requests.
- Faster campaign launch.

### Adoption

Outputs should fit existing revenue workflows and field needs.

Proof language:
- Advisor-ready copy blocks.
- Branch campaign variants.
- Sales talk tracks.
- Exportable context packs.

## Homepage Copy Draft

Headline options:
1. "Governed revenue knowledge for regulated teams."
2. "Turn approved sources into review-ready sales and marketing assets."
3. "The alignment system for regulated revenue teams."

Supporting copy:
> NorthstarIQ helps marketing, sales, enablement, and compliance teams generate cited assets from approved source material, so field teams move faster without losing control of claims, language, or review history.

CTA options:
- "Start a pilot."
- "Request a product walkthrough."
- "Build a source pack."

## Sales Deck Copy Draft

Problem slide:
> Regulated revenue teams are moving faster with AI, but their source material is scattered across decks, folders, call notes, brand guides, and compliance documents. Without source control, AI output creates new review risk.

Solution slide:
> NorthstarIQ gives teams a governed source library, cited answers, and review-ready drafts for battlecards, campaign briefs, objection handlers, and prompt packs.

Proof slide:
> Every output can show source citations, approval status, and review notes.

## Social Copy Guidance

Social posts should not claim the product solves compliance alone. Focus on real workflow pain:
- "Sales is using one version of the story. Marketing has another. Compliance is reviewing a third."
- "The problem with AI content in regulated teams is not the draft. It is the missing source trail."
- "A source-grounded revenue system should make unsupported claims obvious before they hit review."

## Final Rule

If a phrase makes it sound like NorthstarIQ removes human approval, rewrite it. If a phrase makes it clear that NorthstarIQ helps teams create review-ready, source-grounded work, keep it.
