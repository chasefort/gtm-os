## Pricing Summary

The current pricing workstream contains an unresolved tension. The early packaging model includes a $999/month Starter plan for mid-market fintech teams, but recent sales notes suggest enterprise banks expect a scoped annual pilot with a $25k minimum.

The pricing page, sales deck, and proposal template should not present both offers without explaining when each applies.

## Draft Packaging

### Starter

Price:
- $999/month.

Intended buyer:
- One mid-market fintech team.
- Product marketing, enablement, or compliance operations.

Included:
- 10 users.
- 250 uploaded documents.
- Basic cited Q&A.
- Manual Markdown, TXT, and PDF upload.
- Source labels for internal, external, approved, and needs review.
- Basic export.

Risks:
- May frame NorthstarIQ as a generic AI chat subscription.
- May underprice compliance-heavy onboarding.
- May attract buyers who need too much implementation support.

### Growth

Price:
- $2,500/month.

Intended buyer:
- Multiple teams inside a regulated fintech or financial services company.

Included:
- Three teams.
- Review-ready draft generation.
- Shared context packs.
- Approval labels.
- Source categories.
- Standard onboarding.
- Review-ready Markdown exports.

Risks:
- May still be too self-serve for regulated enterprise buyers.
- Requires clear success metrics to justify expansion.

### Regulated Enterprise

Price:
- Custom annual contract.

Intended buyer:
- Regional banks, enterprise banks, wealth management groups, insurance, and other regulated revenue organizations.

Included:
- SSO.
- Audit exports.
- Custom approval workflows.
- Implementation support.
- Priority onboarding.
- Integration planning.
- Compliance workshop.
- Source taxonomy setup.

Risks:
- Longer sales cycle.
- Higher integration expectations.
- Procurement and security review.

## Pilot Offer

Sales notes suggest a $25k annual pilot minimum may be easier to defend than a monthly self-serve plan when the pilot includes implementation, compliance workshops, and success metrics.

Recommended pilot structure:
- 6 to 10 weeks.
- One line of business.
- One primary workflow.
- Source library setup.
- Source taxonomy workshop.
- Compliance language review.
- Template configuration for the selected workflow.
- Baseline and post-pilot ROI measurement.
- Final readout for expansion.

Recommended pilot workflows:
- Advisor social content.
- Branch campaign localization.
- Wealth management sales language.
- Treasury sales objection handling.
- Merger or rebrand messaging consolidation.

## Pricing Tension

The founder memo argues for mid-market fintech because those companies should buy faster. Sales notes show enterprise banks have more urgent pain and are more willing to discuss an annual pilot.

Packaging should not force one motion too early. Recommendation:
- Keep Starter as a future self-serve or low-touch offer.
- Lead current sales conversations with a scoped pilot.
- Use Growth only after the pilot proves repeatable workflow value.

## Value Metrics

Pricing should be tied to measurable workflow outcomes:
- Review-cycle reduction.
- Reuse rate of approved language.
- Reduction in rewrite requests.
- Increase in field-ready assets shipped per month.
- Percent of generated content with approved source citations.
- Number of risky claims flagged before formal review.
- Campaign launch speed.

## Expansion Paths

Potential expansion:
- From one advisor region to national wealth management.
- From one branch campaign to all retail banking campaigns.
- From one product launch to full product marketing source library.
- From sales objection handling to full enablement knowledge base.
- From manual source upload to integrated SharePoint or Seismic sync.

## Discounting Guidance

Avoid discounting below the level required to support onboarding. If the buyer wants a lower price, narrow the workflow rather than discounting the full pilot.

Acceptable scope reductions:
- Fewer source categories.
- One draft type instead of every draft type.
- One line of business.
- Manual export instead of integration support.

Do not remove:
- Compliance source review.
- Citation requirements.
- Baseline ROI measurement.
- Final pilot readout.

## Draft Recommendation

Public-facing pricing should be conservative until the ICP decision is settled. Use:
- "Pilot programs available for regulated revenue teams."
- "Pricing depends on workflow scope, source volume, and review requirements."

Avoid putting "$999/month" on the main website until the team decides whether self-serve is truly part of the launch motion.
