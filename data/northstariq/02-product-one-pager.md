## Product Overview

NorthstarIQ is a governed knowledge workspace for regulated sales and marketing teams. It gives teams a controlled place to upload approved source material, ask questions with citations, and prepare review-ready drafts for campaigns, sales enablement, advisor content, and partner communications.

Current draft language says NorthstarIQ can "automate compliance review" by checking generated material against approved claims, restricted language, and brand rules before content reaches the field. Product marketing should revise this wording before external use. The safer approved phrase is:

> Reduce review cycles while keeping compliance in control.

## Core Capabilities

### Source Library

The Source Library is the central place for materials that revenue teams rely on:
- Product positioning and release notes.
- Pricing and packaging guidance.
- Legal and compliance language.
- Brand voice and messaging rules.
- Sales call notes and objection logs.
- Competitor notes and market research.
- Customer stories and proof points.

Each document should carry basic labels:
- Source type: internal or external.
- Review status: approved or needs review.
- Category: product, sales, customer, competitor, pricing, brand, market, or customer voice.
- Owner.
- Review date or expiration date when available.

### Cited Answers

Users can ask questions such as:
- What can sales say about compliance review?
- Which target buyer is best supported by our current evidence?
- What pricing should be used in an enterprise pilot?
- Which claims are risky or unsupported?
- What should marketing avoid saying?

Answers should cite the underlying material and show whether support comes from an approved internal source, a needs-review internal source, or an external source.

### Review-Ready Drafts

The product can prepare working drafts for common revenue workflows:
- Source-of-truth summaries.
- Sales battlecards.
- Objection responses.
- Campaign briefs.
- Prompt packs for approved language reuse.
- Alignment checks across product, pricing, sales, and brand materials.

Every draft should include citations, call out weak source support, and identify contradictions when materials disagree.

### Approval Trail

Approval Trail keeps source labels, reviewer notes, export history, and citations attached to drafted content. The first release does not replace formal compliance systems of record. It should make review easier by preserving where each claim came from.

## Target Users

Product marketing:
- Builds source packs.
- Prepares launch briefs, messaging pillars, and competitive positioning.

Revenue enablement:
- Creates talk tracks, objection handlers, and seller guidance.
- Identifies outdated or inconsistent field language.

Compliance operations:
- Reviews generated drafts.
- Labels approved claims and restricted phrases.
- Checks citations and review status.

Sales leadership:
- Wants consistent answers for objections, pricing, pilots, and competitors.

Branch or advisor managers:
- Need reusable approved language that can be adapted locally.

## Differentiation

NorthstarIQ should not be positioned as another generic AI writing tool. The differentiation is the workflow around source control:
- Every answer is grounded in source snippets.
- Every source has approval status.
- External material is review-gated.
- Drafts are reusable and exportable.
- The system can flag conflicts across source material.

## Current Product Limits

The current release focuses on source-grounded answer quality and review logic, not full enterprise workflow automation. It does not yet provide production-grade SSO, permissioning, audit exports, publishing integrations, or automated approval routing.

Planned integrations:
- Salesforce.
- Seismic.
- SharePoint.
- Google Drive.
- Slack.

This is a weakness against SecureFlow, which already claims native Salesforce sync, Seismic publishing, SharePoint folder monitoring, and Slack approval notifications.

## Risky Claims Needing Review

The one-pager currently contains claims that should be reviewed before external use:
- "Automates compliance review."
- "Checks generated content against approved claims."
- "Before a campaign, advisor post, or sales asset reaches the field."
- "Approval Trail keeps export history attached to generated content."

These may be directionally true, but product and legal need to confirm exact wording. The approved phrase is "reduces review cycles while keeping compliance in control."

## Recommended Product Narrative

NorthstarIQ helps regulated revenue teams move faster by turning approved source material into cited, review-ready drafts. Marketing and enablement teams can prepare content from a controlled source library, while compliance keeps visibility into the claims, citations, and review status behind each asset.

The system should be described as a governed revenue knowledge workspace, not a compliance automation engine.
